### Promotional text

Capture an object under a moving light and relight it on your iPhone: in 2D, draped on a simulated 3D cloth, or in AR in your own room.

### Description

relightCam turns your iPhone into a scanner for relightable images (RTI, reflectance transformation imaging). Photograph an object under a moving light, then explore it under any light you like: tilt the phone and watch the weave, the gold threads and the folds catch the light.

It's made for historical textiles and samples, and works for any flat object, such as coins, clay tablets, paintings, leather or paper.

CAPTURE
• Put the iPhone on a tripod with a shiny black sphere next to the object, and move a hand-held light between the photos. Focus, exposure and white balance are locked, and an interval timer takes the photos.
• Or import photos from Photos or Files.
• No setup at hand? Try the demo capture of a silk lampas.

PROCESS ON THE PHONE
• The app finds the light direction in every photo from the highlight on the sphere.
• It fits a PTM or HSH relightable image, in the open format of the relight toolkit by the CNR-ISTI Visual Computing Lab.
• Enter the sphere's diameter to give the image its real size.

VIEW
• 2D viewer: drag, or tilt the phone, to move the light. RTI, specular, shaded and normal-map modes.
• 3D cloth viewer: drape the captured textile on a board, on historical dress forms (1770s panniers, 1860s crinoline, 1880s bustle, 1920s) or as free-hanging cloth. Silk, linen, wool and tapestry presets, bias cut, pins, and tiling of a pattern repeat.
• Live AR: hang the simulated cloth in your room with real gravity, light it from the phone itself and walk around it.

EXPORT
• An RTI with a web viewer (OpenLIME), the photos with a .lp light file for RelightLab, or a 3D model (USDZ).

Everything runs on the iPhone: no account, no tracking, no internet connection needed.

Developed at the University of Antwerp. Open source (GPL-3.0): github.com/JoukeVerlinden/relightcam-ios
