# relightCam privacy policy

*Last updated: 4 November 2026*

relightCam is an iPhone app, developed at the University of Antwerp, for capturing and viewing relightable images (RTIs) of objects such as historical textiles.

**relightCam does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.** It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Camera.** relightCam uses the camera to take the photos for an RTI, and, in AR mode, to show the 3D cloth in your room. Photos are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own folder (visible in the Files app under *On My iPhone › relightCam*). The AR camera image is processed on the device and not recorded.
- **Photos you import.** When you import photos, you pick them in Apple's photo picker. relightCam only receives the photos you choose and has no access to the rest of your library.
- **Motion sensors.** The tilt controls read the phone's orientation while the viewer is open. Nothing is stored.
- **On-device processing.** Light detection, RTI fitting, the automatic textile cutout (Apple's Vision framework) and the cloth simulation all run on the iPhone.

## Sharing

relightCam only sends data out of the app when you choose to export or share something (an RTI, photos or a 3D model) with the iOS share sheet. Where that data goes is up to you.

## Deleting your data

Delete a project in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data.

## TestFlight

If you test relightCam through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares your name and email address with the developer, along with crash reports, usage statistics (installs and sessions) and any feedback you send. That data is handled by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and is only used to improve the app.
